# Product Context

## Product name
Class AId

## Product definition
Class AId is a configurable, text-first readiness and learning-assurance platform. It helps learners prepare for structured learning activities before entering advanced, instructor-led or practical sessions.

The word "Lab" represents any structured learning or practice environment, including computing, engineering, materials, sciences, language learning, professional training, cybersecurity, data science and business simulations.

## Core problem
Providing manuals, videos and learning resources does not confirm that learners understand foundational concepts, remember the correct procedure, recognise misconceptions, understand safety or compliance requirements, or know whether they are ready to progress.

Instructors therefore spend significant time repeating foundational material instead of focusing on advanced concepts, application and hands-on guidance.

## Product outcomes
1. Improve learner confidence and cognitive readiness.
2. Improve safety, compliance and responsible-practice awareness.
3. Reduce repetitive foundational instruction via adaptive learning.
4. Help instructors focus on advanced learning and hands-on support.
5. Auto-generate assessment content from uploaded source material.
6. Support reusable learning modules across different domains.

## User layers

### Learner
- View assigned modules.
- Follow adaptive guided activities with comprehension checkpoints.
- Ask source-grounded questions to AI coach (with citations).
- Complete readiness assessments (server-scored, critical rules enforced).
- Receive immediate feedback, competency scores, and targeted remediation.

### Instructor
- Upload source documents for AI processing.
- Review and approve auto-generated questions.
- Configure competency weights, thresholds, and critical flags.
- View cohort readiness, misconceptions, step friction, and intervention list.

### Administrator
- Manage module ownership, versions and publication status.
- Monitor usage, audit records, and governance compliance.

## Product boundaries
- Does not replace instructors or certify physical competence.
- Does not allow AI to determine authoritative pass/fail status.
- Does not allow unapproved source content to be published.
- Readiness is advisory within the configured assessment scope.
- The platform provides the engine, not a universal formula.
