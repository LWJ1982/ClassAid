Learner Guide

Your path from assigned module to proven readiness.

1 Dashboard

Your central hub showing all assigned modules and your current progress across each one.

  • View modules assigned to you by your instructor
  • See completion status (Not Started, In Progress, Ready, Not Ready)
  • Track overall progress across all modules

2 Module Overview

Before starting a module, review what is expected of you.

  • Read the module objectives and learning outcomes
  • Check any prerequisites you need to have completed
  • Review the compliance notice and readiness criteria

3 Guided Activity

Step through the module content at a pace that ensures comprehension. Adaptive checkpoints verify understanding at each stage.

  • Read each step carefully - a minimum read time is enforced
  • Answer comprehension checkpoints to proceed to the next step
  • If you answer incorrectly, you will be looped back to review the content before retrying
  • You must pass each checkpoint before advancing
Take your time. The minimum read time exists to ensure genuine engagement with the material, not to slow you down artificially.

4 AI Coach

Ask questions about the module content and receive answers grounded in approved source materials.

  • Type any question related to the module content
  • Receive answers with citations from the approved source documents
  • Use this to clarify concepts before attempting the assessment
The AI Coach draws only from instructor-approved sources. Answers are for explanation only and do not affect your readiness score.

5 Assessment

Demonstrate your readiness with a 5-question assessment scored entirely server-side.

  • Answer all 5 questions to the best of your ability
  • All scoring happens on the server - no client-side manipulation possible
  • Some questions may be flagged as critical - getting these wrong can block your Ready status regardless of overall score

6 Readiness Report

View your results and understand exactly where you stand.

  • See your overall readiness score
  • Review competency breakdown by topic area
  • Identify any critical failures that blocked readiness
  • Follow suggested remediation actions to improve

Instructor Guide

Configure modules, monitor cohort progress, and intervene where needed.

1 Cohort Insights

A real-time overview of your cohort's readiness status and performance patterns.

  • See how many learners are assigned, started, and completed
  • View readiness distribution (Ready vs Not Ready vs In Progress)
  • Review competency averages across all learners
  • Identify step friction - which steps cause the most retries

2 Module Configuration

Fine-tune assessment criteria to match your standards.

  • Set the overall readiness threshold (50-100%)
  • Adjust competency weights to emphasise critical areas
  • Set minimum thresholds per competency
  • Mark questions as mandatory or critical (safety-blocking)
Critical flags mean a learner cannot achieve Ready status if they fail that question, regardless of their total score. Use this for safety-critical knowledge.

3 Content & AI

Upload source materials and let AI generate assessment questions from your content.

  • Upload source documents (PDF, TXT, MD formats supported)
  • AI automatically generates questions based on uploaded content
  • Generated questions appear in the approval queue
  • Source documents feed the AI Coach RAG system

4 Checkpoint Approval

Review and curate AI-generated questions before they reach learners.

  • Review each auto-generated question for accuracy and relevance
  • Approve, reject, or edit questions as needed
  • Set minimum read times for guided activity steps
  • Assign competency tags and critical/mandatory flags

5 Misconceptions

See ranked wrong answers with frequency counts to understand common gaps.

  • View the most frequently chosen wrong answers
  • Identify systematic misunderstandings across your cohort
  • Use insights to update content or add clarifying material

6 Intervention List

A prioritised list of learners who need your attention before the session.

  • Critical failures appear at the top (safety-related gaps)
  • See each learner's specific weak areas and failure points
  • Plan targeted interventions before the practical session
  • Track whether previously flagged learners have improved

Admin Guide

Manage modules, ensure governance compliance, and maintain audit trails.

1 Module Registry

A complete inventory of all modules in the system with full metadata.

  • View all modules with title, domain, owner, and version
  • Check source version and current status (Draft, Published, Archived)
  • See published date, next review date, and usage statistics
  • Filter and search across the full module catalogue

2 Governance Warnings

Proactive alerts for modules that may not meet governance standards.

  • Draft modules that have been in draft state too long are flagged
  • Modules with missing or incomplete metadata are identified
  • Modules past their review date are highlighted
  • Take action to resolve governance issues before they become problems

3 Audit Trail

A complete, immutable log of all significant actions in the system.

  • Every publish, approve, and source update is logged
  • Threshold changes and configuration modifications are recorded
  • Each entry includes the actor, timestamp, and full details
  • Filter by action type, actor, module, or date range
The audit trail is append-only. Records cannot be modified or deleted, ensuring full traceability for compliance requirements.
